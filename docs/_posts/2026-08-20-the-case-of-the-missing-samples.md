Rich Olivieri from [FB Crumar Seven & Seventeen Users](https://www.facebook.com/groups/984650751685043)
reported a bug where samples he had installed on his Seven were showing up as
"installed, not in catalogue". So, the samples were 'there' but not there.

But the explanation was simple: I only had 7 of the 10 available samples installed
on my Seven. I installed the final 3 samples this morning and now everything is
working properly.

![The Expansions list before the fix: Venice Grand C5, Venice Grand CFX and Venice Upright K8 each appear twice — once marked Unverified, and again lower down marked "Installed, not in the catalogue"](/assets/img/case-of-the-missing-samples.png)

To get the fix: quit Seven11 completely (Cmd-Q — closing the window isn't enough)
and reopen it. It'll pull 1.2.1 down on its own. Case solved!
